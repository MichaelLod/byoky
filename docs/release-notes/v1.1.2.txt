A security update.
• Extension: keys, trusted sites and limits are tamper-protected; tampering pauses apps until you resume them. Older versions can't open the upgraded wallet. Long requests no longer break off.
• A token limit of 0 now blocks an app.
• Phones hide the wallet in the app switcher; Android blocks screenshots of password and key screens.
• Routing, in-wallet browser, screen reader and large-text fixes.
Bridge and plugin users: update both, then set their limits and groups again.
